## Model outputs

Parts of `data/` are, or are derived from, text generated by third-party models: the `output`
column of `data/full_results/*.csv`, `data/surface_vs_behaviour/recursion_real_outputs.csv`,
and the scores and first-token distributions computed from them. The CC BY 4.0 grant covers
only the authors' own rights in these files. Use of the generated text is also subject to the
terms of the model that produced it:

| Model(s) | Upstream licence |
|---|---|
| `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B`, `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` | [Llama 3.1 Community License Agreement](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B/blob/main/LICENSE) and the Llama 3.1 Acceptable Use Policy |
| `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B`, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B`, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` | Apache License 2.0 |
| `distilgpt2` (`distilbert/distilgpt2`) | Apache License 2.0 |
| `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (embedder used for *s*; it generated no text) | Apache License 2.0 |

The licences above are those stated on each model's Hugging Face model card (checked
26 October 2026). The Llama 3.1 licence asks redistributors of Llama materials to include
"Built with Llama" and a copy of the licence; check its current terms before redistributing
the Llama outputs.
